## How We Engage

> *We don't sell hours or stack credentials. We solve problems with defined scope, direct communication, and measurable outcomes. If a problem is outside our competence, we say so.*

Every engagement follows a simple structure:

1. **Brief email or call** — you describe the problem, we ask the right questions
2. **Scope clarification** — written, agreed, no surprises
3. **Delivery** — regular checkpoints, no ghost periods
4. **Handover** — documentation, access, full clarity on what was built and why
